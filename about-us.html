About EasyCashbook

A simple cashbook and notebook app that works everywhere — online or offline.

EasyCashbook was built for people who need to track daily income, expenses, and notes without friction — whether you're a small shop owner, a freelancer, or just someone managing personal finances. No internet connection, no jargon, no complicated accounting software required.

What EasyCashbook does

Cashbook

Log income and expenses by category, track running balances, and view summaries at a glance.

Notebooks

Keep quick notes and reminders alongside your financial records, organized into notebooks.

Works offline

Every entry is saved to your device instantly — no internet required to use the app day to day.

Auto-sync

When you're back online, your data syncs automatically in the background — no manual backup needed.

Our approach

We believe everyday financial tracking shouldn't depend on a perfect internet connection or a steep learning curve. EasyCashbook is designed offline-first: your data lives on your device first, and cloud sync is a convenience layer on top — not a requirement to get things done.

Our commitment

  • We never sell your personal data or your cashbook/notebook content to third parties.
  • We collect only what's needed to run your account and sync your data — no more.
  • We're upfront about our infrastructure and its limitations — see our Privacy Policy and Terms for details.
