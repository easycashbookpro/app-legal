Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DD Month YYYY]  •  Version 1.1

EasyCashbook ("we", "our", "the app") is a cashbook and notebook app developed by [Your Name / Company Name]. This policy explains what information we collect, how it's used, how it's stored, and your rights.

1. Account information you provide

To create an account, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored as a one-way hash — we never store or can see your actual password)

This information is used solely to create and secure your account, identify you when you sign in, and enable sync across your devices. We do not use it for marketing, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

2. Your cashbook & notebook data

Your cashbook entries, categories, and notebook notes are stored:

  • Locally on your device at all times, so the app works fully offline, and
  • Synced automatically to our server database whenever your device has an internet connection, so your data is backed up and available across devices.

If you're offline, changes are saved locally and sync automatically in the background the next time you're connected — nothing is lost due to lack of internet.

3. How your data is hosted

Our backend runs on third-party cloud infrastructure (application hosting and a managed database provider). These providers host and transmit data on our behalf and do not use it for their own purposes, sell it, or share it with advertisers. We do not sell or rent your personal data or your cashbook/notebook content to any third party, for any purpose.

4. Permissions we request

  • Camera — only if you attach a photo/receipt to a cashbook entry.
  • Storage / Media — to export/import backups (e.g. CSV/PDF) or attach images.

5. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and our servers, and password hashing so your actual password is never stored in readable form.

Honest disclosure: No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. We continually work to improve our security practices as the app grows.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only:

  • With infrastructure providers strictly to host and run the app (see Section 3)
  • When you explicitly export or share a report/entry yourself
  • If required by law

7. Your rights & account deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In-app: Settings → Account → Delete My Account
  • Online: email us at the address below

Deleting your account permanently removes your synced data from our servers. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear app data.

8. Children's privacy

EasyCashbook is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date above and, where appropriate, an in-app notice.
